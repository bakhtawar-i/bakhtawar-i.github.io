I am a Machine Learning Engineer applying for PhD positions for the 2026–27 cycle. I completed my MSc in Artificial Intelligence at NUST Pakistan in 2025 and my BSc in Electrical Engineering at GIKI, Pakistan in 2020.

My research interests lie in robotics and embodied AI including vision-language-action (VLA) systems, computer vision for medical imaging, and clinical and multimodal healthcare AI spanning EEG, pathology, and EHR. Across these areas, I'm drawn to work on where models quietly fail — recent projects include layer-wise probing of a VLA policy in robotic manipulation and seizure detection from EEG.

I'm currently on the market for PhD positions and open to opportunities across these areas. If my background looks like a fit for your lab, I'd love to connect — email me.

Publications

Action Space Design and Policy Optimisation for Reinforcement Learning-Based UAV Collision Avoidance in Post-Earthquake Environments (preprint submitted to Elsevier)
Bakhtawar Iftikhar, Rizwan Ahmad, Salman Ghafoor, Shams Qazi. A controlled factorial study (PPO/SAC × discrete/continuous/hybrid action spaces × three environment densities) for UAV collision avoidance in simulated post-earthquake debris fields. SAC fails catastrophically on hybrid action spaces (0% success rate in open environments) — traced to its single-parameter entropy auto-tuning mechanism, which cannot independently regulate the joint discrete-continuous entropy landscape.

Selected Projects

eeg-seizure-fusion-probe
Seizure detection on CHB-MIT with LaBraM fine-tuning. Addressing severe class imbalance (0.1–0.6% seizure windows) with sensitivity, AUC-PR, and false-alarms-per-hour as primary metrics rather than accuracy. In progress —
vla-grounding-probe
Layer-wise probing of SmolVLA on LIBERO-Goal tasks. Language embedding similarity was lowest for paraphrases and highest for object-swaps and negations — the inverse of grounded conditioning — replicated in 4/7 tasks and cross-checked against the CLIP-family affirmation-bias literature.
Multimodal Agentic RL System
Diagnosed FiLM vs. concat fusion failure modes in a language-conditioned RL policy. Naive fusion caused language input to be silently ignored, and policy confidence was uninformative for detecting failure (∼0.90 on wrong pickups vs. 0.92–0.96 on correct ones).
IB-AstroPT
Information Bottleneck analysis of the AstroPT transformer. Clear depth-dependent fitting signal, no resolvable compression — a null result, validated against a positive control.
QuantVol
HAR-RV vs. LightGBM/XGBoost for volatility forecasting. The simple econometric baseline won; Optuna-tuned hyperparameters underperformed defaults in walk-forward evaluation.
